# Eve, AgentOS, and Rivet stack focus

QuantFlow will park Mastra for the Dock Runtime rework and focus the stack on Eve agents, AgentOS, and Rivet Actors. Eve is the preferred custom-agent authoring framework, AgentOS is the preferred runtime for talkable agent sessions, and Rivet Actors are the preferred candidate for future durable TileActor supervision. QuantFlow remains the product and truth owner; these systems support actor definition, execution, and supervision.
